How to use this page

  • Open Skeletal Plan and tap the scan button in any implant modal or the Case Scan modal.
  • Camera scan: point the in-app camera at a barcode on this page (zoom to ~200% for small codes).
  • Wedge / HID scanner: focus the text-entry field in the scan modal, then scan the barcode.
  • Manual entry: type a GTIN from the table below directly into the scan modal's GTIN field.

These are publicly-assigned manufacturer UDI codes (GUDID / EUDAMED). This page contains no patient data and makes no clinical claims.

Medtronic

Solera — GS1 DataMatrix (GTIN + lot + expiry)

ModuLeX — modular trio (shank / head / set screw)

Scan all three into the same screw slot in the ModuLeX modal. Each is a separate trade item with its own lot and expiry.

Cross-system block test: scan the ModuLeX Head GTIN (20763000608105) while a Xia 3 modal is open. The app must display a "Component not compatible" dialog — not silently switch systems.

Stryker

Xia 3 — GS1 DataMatrix

Xia 3 — HIBC two-part (primary + secondary)

Scan the primary first (product identity), then the secondary (lot + expiry) within 5 seconds. The app merges them into one UdiRecord.

ES2 — three-path test (all resolve to the same implant)

HIBC primary, EU sterile GTIN, and US non-sterile GTIN all identify the same ES2 polyaxial Ø8.5×50 mm cannulated screw. The US GTIN additionally resolves bladeLength = long.
